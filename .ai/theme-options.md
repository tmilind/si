# SourceIntellect Theme Options

Three distinct directions — all light, clean, and minimal with different accent personalities.

---

## Option A: "Signal"
**Warm Coral — Approachable & Distinctive**

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Ink (text) | Deep navy-black | `#1a1a2e` |
| Graphite (body) | Warm gray | `#4a4a5c` |
| Mist (background) | Off-white | `#f7f7f9` |
| **Accent** | Warm coral | `#e85d4c` |
| Accent light | Coral tint | `#fef0ee` |

**Fonts:** Inter (headers & body)

**Personality:** Warm, human, stands out in tech space. Says "we're experts, but we're friendly."

## Shared Elements (All Options)

- Light, clean backgrounds
- Generous white space
- Geometric, minimal aesthetic
- Single accent color (used sparingly)
- Modern sans-serif typography
- Professional but approachable tone
