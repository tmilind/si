The [model context protocol](https://example.com/mcp) now ships with `stdio` and HTTP transports, which quietly settles the question of where tool servers live. Most teams will run them next to the agent rather than behind a gateway, and that choice shapes everything from auth to latency. The interesting part is what this does to vendor lock-in over the next year.
