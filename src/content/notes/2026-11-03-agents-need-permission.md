Websites will treat AI agents as a negotiation, not a given. MIT Technology Review reports that Amazon blocked Meta's Muse agent soon after its September launch, and with AI summaries already cutting click-throughs from search, we expect more sites to follow. If you build agents over documents, build on sources you have a clear right to use: your own files, licensed feeds, APIs with terms. The open web makes a good demo and a fragile dependency.
